'''Naomi Mulitauaopele''' (born March 6, 1976) is an American former professional [[basketball]] player.

==High school==
Mulitauaopele played basketball at [[Chief Sealth International High School]] in [[Seattle]], [[Washington (state)|Washington]]. Coming out of high school, she was considered the state's top player.

==International career==
After the WNBA, Mulitauaopele played professionally in Turkey, Korea, and Spain.

==Personal life==
