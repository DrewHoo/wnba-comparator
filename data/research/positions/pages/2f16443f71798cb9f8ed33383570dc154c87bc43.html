Career Highlights
Averaged 20.3 points and 9.2 rebounds for ASA Jerusalem in Israel during 1996-97 season
First player in NCAA history to record 2,500 points, grab 1,000 rebounds and register 300 steals
All-time leader in scoring and steals in Big West history
All-time leading rebounder (10.0) in New Mexico State history
Named AP All-America as a junior (1994-95)
Honorable Mention Kodak All-America (1994-96)
Three-time Big West Player of the Year (1994-96)
Four-time Big West Conference First Team selection
Named Big West Conference Player of the Week a record 13 times
Big West Freshman of the Year
In 119 career games, averaged 21.9 points, 10.0 rebounds, 2.83 steals and shot .564 from the field

WNBA Transactions
Selected by the Cleveland Rockers in the fourth round (29th overall) of the WNBA Draft, 04/28/97 Moved to the Rockers' developmental roster, 6/24/97 Moved to the Rockers' active roster, 7/3/97
