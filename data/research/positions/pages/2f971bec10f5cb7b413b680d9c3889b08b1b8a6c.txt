'''Marcie Alberts''' (born April 12, 1975) is an American former professional [[basketball]] player and current [[Coach (basketball)|coach]]. Alberts participated in the inaugural season of the [[WNBA]].

==College career==
Alberts played for [[Ohio State University]] during her college athletic career from 1993 to 1997.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://nl.newsbank.com/nl-search/we/Archives?p_product=AK&s_site=ohio&p_multi=AK&p_theme=realcities&p_action=search&p_maxdocs=200&p_topdoc=1&p_text_direct-0=0EB6BF8A6E4A784A&p_field_direct-0=document_id&p_perpage=10&p_sort=YMD_date:D&s_trackval=GooglePM|title=Beacon Journal:  Search Results|website=nl.newsbank.com|access-date=2017-09-26}}</ref> Her overall team's record through her college years was 64 - 56. She had 91.3% of her free-throw attempts in 1996–97, the highest recorded free-throw percentage for an Ohio State senior.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://grfx.cstv.com/photos/schools/osu/sports/w-baskbl/auto_pdf/2016-17/misc_non_event/1617FULLguide.pdf|title=Ohio State Media Guide|access-date=2017-09-26|archive-date=2017-09-26|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170926235658/http://grfx.cstv.com/photos/schools/osu/sports/w-baskbl/auto_pdf/2016-17/misc_non_event/1617FULLguide.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> She made 203 during her OSU years which was the 2nd-highest 3-pointers made in a Buckeye's career at the time.<ref>https://ohiobasketballhalloffame.com/hall-of-fame/inductees/2016/marcie-alberts.html</ref>

Alberts graduated from OSU in 1997 with a [[Bachelor of Science|Bachelor's of Science degree]].

==WNBA==
After graduating from OSU, although she would not be selected in the draft, Alberts was still able to play in the [[Women's National Basketball Association|WNBA]]'s inaugural season for the [[Cleveland Rockers]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.heidelberg.edu/athletics/teams/wbasketball/coaches/alberts|title=Marcie Alberts {{!}} Heidelberg University|date=2012-06-23|access-date=2017-09-26|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120623225941/http://www.heidelberg.edu/athletics/teams/wbasketball/coaches/alberts|archive-date=2012-06-23}}</ref> Her debut game would be played on July 7, 1997, in an 81 - 70 win over the [[Los Angeles Sparks]] where she recorded 1 assist and no other stats in 7 minutes of playing time.<ref>https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/boxscores/199707070LAS.html</ref>

Alberts' WNBA career would be incredibly short, as she only played 5 games with the Rockers in 1997 and these ended up being her only 5 games in the league. Her 5th and final game was played on July 15, 1997, where the Rockers would lose to the [[New York Liberty]] 59 - 76 with Alberts recording 1 assist, 1 rebound and 1 steal, but no points.<ref>https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/boxscores/199707150NYL.html</ref>

In her 5 WNBA career games, Alberts did not score a single point (missing both of her only 2 field goals attempts and never attempting a free throw) and had totals of 30 minutes playing time, 1 rebound, 3 assists and 2 steals.

==Coaching career==
She was head coach of [[Orrville High School]]'s girls basketball team, earning a 117 - 45 record during her tenure. She then became the women's head basketball coach at [[Heidelberg College]].<ref name=":0" /> After not being retained following the 2012-2013 basketball season, Alberts was hired in July 2013 to become head girls basketball coach at Mount Vernon High School in Mount Vernon, Ohio. In August 2013, Alberts joined the NCAA Division I Women's Basketball coaching staff as an assistant coach for [[IPFW]].<ref name="IPFW">{{cite web|url=http://www.gomastodons.com/news/2013/9/10/Tickets_0910131947.aspx|title=Women's Basketball Adds Two to Staff|publisher=SIDEARM SPORTS|access-date=9 September 2013}}</ref> She was promoted to first assistant before the 2018-2019 basketball season.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Marcie Alberts - Assistant Coach - Women's Basketball Coaches |url=https://gomastodons.com/sports/womens-basketball/roster/coaches/marcie--alberts/1159 |access-date=2026-08-29 |website=Purdue Fort Wayne Athletics |language=en}}</ref>

She was inducted into the Ohio Basketball Hall of Fame on May 21, 2016, at the 11th Annual Ceremony in Columbus.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://ohiobasketballhalloffame.com/hall-of-fame/inductees/2016/marcie-alberts.html|title=Ohio Basketball Hall of Fame : Hall of Fame : Inductees : 2016 : Marcie Alberts|website=ohiobasketballhalloffame.com|language=en|access-date=2017-09-26}}</ref>
