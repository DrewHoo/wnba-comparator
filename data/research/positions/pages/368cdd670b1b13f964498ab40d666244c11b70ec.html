Career Highlights
Did not pursue professional career after college due to reconstructive surgery to her right shoulder in 1993
Three-year starter at USC
Missed three weeks with a shoulder separation in her senior season, but still averaged 5.7 points, 5.1 rebounds and shot .485 from the floor
Battled ankle problems during her junior season
A member of the Pac-10 All-Stars in 1990
Played three seasons with Los Angeles Sparks center Lisa Leslie at USC
Scored over 700 points in collegiate career

WNBA Transactions
Selected as a developmental player and assigned to Los Angeles Sparks, 05/27/97 Moved to Sparks' active roster, 08/15/97

Personal
Received a B.S. in Accounting from USC
Has been working at the Southern California offices of the accounting firm Coopers and Lybrand L.L.P. for three and a half years as an auditor
Also likes golf, soccer, volleyball, softball and inline skating
Has done community work with the hospital where her mother works as a nurse and at health clinics
Favorite food is steak
Favorite music is hard rock
Plans to return to her career in accounting and would eventually like to become a controller
