Career Highlights
Played 1985 season for Avon-Acala in Spain
A four-year starter at Ohio State University
Led Buckeyes in scoring (14.1 ppg), assists (5.3 apg), steals (3.7) and minutes played (32.6 mpg)
Set school record for assists in a single season (167) and a single game (14), both set as a junior in 1984
Member of the 1981 U.S. Junior National Team and the 1982 Women's Select Team

WNBA Transactions
Signed as a developmental player and assigned to the Sacramento Monarchs, 05/27/97 Moved to the Monarchs' active roster, 7/16/97

Personal
Hobbies include martial arts
Coached the Bishop Montgomery High School (Torrance, Calif.) to two state Division III championships in three years
Aside from coaching and playing, also has a career at Simon & Schuster Publishing Company as a sales representative
Has a cocker spaniel named Dori
Favorite food is her mom's homemade vegetable soup
Would like to pursue coaching and establish her own sports-related business following playing career
Most proud achievement is receiving her master's degree in Physical Education
