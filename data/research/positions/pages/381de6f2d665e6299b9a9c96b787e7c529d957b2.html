Career Highlights
Averaged 16.8 points and 6.1 rebounds per game and was selected to the All-SEC second team in 1996-97
In 1996-97 played in 28 games, earning 26 starts
Scored career-high 32 points against Auburn, including a career-best seven three-pointers, which were the most in the SEC that season and the most in LSU history
Led SEC in free throw shooting percentage (.840)
In just two seasons with LSU, notched 990 points and ranks among the top 15 in LSU scoring history
Sixth-best scoring average (16.8) in LSU history
Established LSU record by making 21 consecutive free throws to open the season as a junior
During the 1995-96 campaign, was named to the SEC All-Tournament team and the Women's NIT All-Tournament team
