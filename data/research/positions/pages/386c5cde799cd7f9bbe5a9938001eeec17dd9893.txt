'''Megan Helen Compain''' (born 18 September 1975) is a New Zealand former [[basketball]] player who competed in the [[2000 Summer Olympics]] and in the [[2004 Summer Olympics]].<ref name="sref">{{cite Sports-Reference |url=https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/co/megan-compain-1.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200418031354/https://www.sports-reference.com/olympics/athletes/co/megan-compain-1.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=18 April 2020|access-date=12 July 2012}}</ref> As of 2024, she was a Basketball New Zealand board member.<ref>[https://nz.basketball/about-us/board-members/ Meet our Board]</ref> She played for the [[Utah Starzz]] during the 1997 season, becoming the first New Zealander to play in the WNBA.<ref>Tunnicliffe, Bridget - [https://www.msn.com/en-nz/news/national/proud-moment-megan-compain-hails-charlisse-leger-walker-s-historic-wnba-draft/ar-AA20PNCW 'Proud moment': Megan Compain hails Charlisse Leger-Walker’s historic WNBA draft]. Radio New Zealand (RNZ via msn.com), April 14, 2026.</ref>

==Biography==
She was born 18 September 1975 in [[Whanganui]], New Zealand.<ref name="Olympedia">{{cite web |title=Megan Compain |url=http://www.olympedia.org/athletes/89816 |publisher=Olympedia |access-date=25 June 2021}}</ref> As an exchange student she attended [[Middle Township High School]] in New Jersey.<ref>Staff. [http://www.pressofatlanticcity.com/test/article_927cb8b0-1d24-515c-a7a5-181efb854de4.html "New Jersey Olympians in other sports"], ''[[The Press of Atlantic City]]'', 28 August 2009. Accessed 22 September 2011. "Megan Compain. Sport: Basketball (New Zealand) – New Jersey Ties: Compain attended Middle Township High School as an exchange student through 1993 and played for the girls' basketball team during her junior and senior years."</ref> She competed in the [[2000 Summer Olympics]] and in the [[2004 Summer Olympics]].
