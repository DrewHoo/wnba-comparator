'''Toccara Williams''' is a former basketball [[point guard]] who played for the [[WNBA]]'s [[San Antonio Silver Stars]] in the [[2004 WNBA season|2004 season]]. Prior to playing in the WNBA, she played [[college basketball]] at [[Texas A&M Aggies women's basketball|Texas A&M]] from 2000 to 2004.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.wnba.com/draft2004/prospect_toccara_williams.html|title=Toccara Williams, Texas A&M|publisher=WNBA.com|accessdate=2008-03-15}}</ref> In her senior year, she led the nation for steals per game among Division I teams with an average of 4.1 steals per game.<ref name=":0" /> She was then selected in the third round of the [[2004 WNBA draft]]. Toccara also played professionally overseas in Turkey, Korea, China, and Israel. Williams now has a non profit organization called Sweet Rebound that is geared towards the youth by utilizing sports as a platform for education.

She is one of only two [[NCAA Division I]] women's players to have recorded a steal in each game of her career, and is the only one of the two to have also recorded an assist in each of her college games.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/w_basketball_RB/2021/D1.pdf |title=Individual Records: Steals |work=2020–21 NCAA Division I Women's Basketball Records |publisher=NCAA |page=3 |accessdate=September 18, 2020}}</ref>
