Career Highlights
Played 1996-97 season for Como in Italy
Averaged 9.2 points and 6.2 rebounds for the season
In 1994, moved to Como and has played three years there
Played for Cesena from 1989-94
Won silver medals at the 1993 and 1995 European Championships
Also played in the 1985, 1987, 1989 and 1991 European Cup championships
Has captured six league and seven European Cup championships
Has played in two Olympic Games (1992 and 1996) and one World Championship (1994) for Italy
Has played professional since 1989
Played one season for the University of Texas (1988-89), averaging 8.3 points, five rebounds and 2.3 assists in 12 games
Season cut short when she suffered arthroscopic surgery in December 1988, mMissed 19 games, but returned to the lineup in mid-February
Voted Rookie of the Year for the Italian National Team in 1983

WNBA Transactions
Selected in the fourth round (32nd overall) by the Houston Comets in the WNBA Draft, 04/28/97

Personal
Left her pre-med study to join Cesena in 1989
Her fiance, Sergio Ghisleni, is one of the top sportswriters in Italy, writing for Gazzetta della Sport
Sergio has taken a leave of absence to accompany her to the United States, so she won't be lonely
Enjoys skiing, biking, listening to rock music and repairing broken things in her spare time
Very proud that she is mechanically inclined and can fix cars, toasters and CD players
Speaks Italian, English, a Venetian dialect, German and a little French
Wants to be a doctor or physical therapist when her playing career is over
Favorite book is The Hobbit by J.R.R. Tolkien
