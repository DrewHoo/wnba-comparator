'''Gabriela Mărginean''' (born 12 February 1987 in [[Cluj-Napoca]]) is a Romanian professional [[women's basketball]] player who plays for the Turkey club [[İzmit Belediyespor]].

==College career==
Mărginean was a two-sport athlete at [[Drexel University]] and led the NCAA in made free throws in her sophomore season. Her 2,581 career points while at Drexel makes her Philadelphia's highest scoring women's basketball player. This point total also med her the highest scoring athlete in the history of the [[Colonial Athletic Association|CAA]] until 2013, when she was surpassed by [[Elena Delle Donne]].<ref>{{cite web|date=May 20, 2010 |title=Ambler and Marginean Named Semanik Award Winners at the Best of the Blue and Gold Banquet |url=http://www.drexeldragons.com/news/2010/5/20/WBB_0520102722.aspx?path=mlax |publisher=Drexel Dragons |access-date=2010-06-10}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|date=April 9, 2010 |title=Drexel's Marginean drafted by WNBA's Minnesota Lynx |url=http://www.philly.com/dailynews/sports/20100409_Drexel_s_Marginean_drafted_by_WNBA_s_Minnesota_Lynx.html#axzz0qSmIGwM9 |first=Mike |last=Mazzeo |work=Philadelphia Daily News |access-date=2010-06-10 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100411232207/http://www.philly.com/dailynews/sports/20100409_Drexel_s_Marginean_drafted_by_WNBA_s_Minnesota_Lynx.html |archive-date=April 11, 2010 }}</ref>

While playing for the [[Drexel Dragons]] women's basketball team Mărginean played in 125 games and averaged 20.6 points and 7.6 rebounds per game. Marginean led Drexel to its first-ever NCAA Tournament appearance in 2009.<ref>{{cite news |date=May 20, 2010 |title=Overcoming the Odds, Marginean Makes the Team |url=http://www.sportspagemagazine.com/content/bb/wp-bb/art-wp-bb/overcoming-the-odds-marginean-makes-the-team.shtml?40342 |first=Jeffrey |last=Williams |work=Sports Page Magazine |access-date=2010-06-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110716122151/http://www.sportspagemagazine.com/content/bb/wp-bb/art-wp-bb/overcoming-the-odds-marginean-makes-the-team.shtml?40342 |archive-date=2011-07-16 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Professional career==

===WNBA===
Mărginean was drafted to the [[Minnesota Lynx]], the 26th pick of the [[2010 WNBA draft]]. In May the Lynx activated [[Rebekkah Brunson]] and Mărginean was waived from the roster.<ref>{{cite news|date=May 24, 2010 |title=Lynx reinstate Brunson, waive Marginean |url=http://www.kansascity.com/2010/05/24/1967903/lynx-reinstate-brunson-waive-marginean.html |work=The Kansas City Star |access-date=2010-06-10}} {{Dead link|date=October 2010|bot=H3llBot}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|date=May 25, 2010 |title=Sports in Brief: Fla. court rules against NCAA |url=http://www.philly.com/inquirer/sports/20100525_Sports_in_Brief__Fla__court_rules_against_NCAA.html#axzz0qSgBd7LQ |work=The Philadelphia Inquirer |access-date=2010-06-10}}</ref> She is the second [[Romania]]n who has played in the [[WNBA]] after [[Florina Pașcalău]] who was the player of [[Seattle Storm]] in 2008.

===Europe===
Later she played for [[Panathinaikos Women BC|Panathinaikos Athens]]. Her stats read 17.3 points, 7.6 boards, 2.2 steals and 1.5 assists per game. Eurobasket.com named her ''All-Greek League Bosman Player of the Year''.<ref>{{cite news|date=July 7, 2012 |title=CSM Targoviste add Gabriela Marginean and Florina Pascalau - |url=https://www.eurobasket.com/Euroleague/basketball.aspx?NewsID=277571&Women=1 |work=EuroBasket |access-date=2012-07-14}}</ref>

Gabriela Mărginean joined [[ASPTT Arras|Arras Pays d'Artois]] in the summer of 2010. She appeared 26 times in [[Ligue Féminine de Basketball]] averaging 12.3 points and 3.6 rebounds. She managed to win in the 2012 French Cup handing a 58–64 loss to [[CJM Bourges Basket|Bourges]] to take the trophy. Mărginean was one of the best Arras players in the final. She scored 11 points and pulled down 6 rebounds for 35 minutes on the court. Thanks to this victory Arras also booked a spot for the [[EuroLeague Women 2012–13]] season. In [[EuroCup Women 2011–12|EuroCup]], Marginean added 16.9 ppg and 4.8 rpg for 10 games.<ref name=fra>{{cite news|date=May 21, 2012 |title=Gabriela Marginean wins French Cup with Arras |url=http://www.eurobasket.com/news_print.asp?NewsID=272093 |work=eurobasket.com |access-date=2012-07-13}}</ref>

On July 7, 2012, it was reported that the Romanian champions [[CSM Târgovişte]] have signed Gabriela Mărginean.<ref name=LWB />
