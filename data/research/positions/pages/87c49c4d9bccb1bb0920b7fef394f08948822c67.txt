'''Nyree Khadijah Roberts''' (born March 10, 1976) is an American professional [[women's basketball]] player. 

She was raised in [[Jersey City, New Jersey]],<ref name=":0" /> where she attended and played prep basketball at [[St. Anthony High School (New Jersey)|St. Anthony High School]].<ref>[https://www.nydailynews.com/1997/03/30/from-jersey-city-to-glory-nyree-now-noticed-at-odu/ "From Jersey City To Glory Nyree Now Noticed At ODU"], ''[[New York Daily News]]'', March 30, 1997. Accessed July 22, 2024. "She comes from the land of Hurleys, St. Anthony High School in Jersey City. Except that Nyree Roberts is from the other side of the gym divider, from the girls' program."</ref>

As a forward/center at [[Old Dominion University]], Roberts was named to the Final Four All Tournament team in 1997.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.odusports.com/ViewArticle.dbml?DB_OEM_ID=31100&ATCLID=208424711|title=Player Bio: Nyree Roberts|website=odusports.com|access-date=2016-04-12}}</ref><ref name="FF Record book"/> 

Roberts played in the [[WNBA]] from 1998 to 1999 as a member of the [[Houston Comets]] and [[Washington Mystics]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/players/r/roberny01w.html|title=Nyree Roberts WNBA Stats|website=Basketball-Reference.com|access-date=2016-04-12}}</ref> During the [[2000 WNBA expansion draft|2000 expansion draft]] on December 15, 1999, Roberts was selected by the [[Indiana Fever]] but ultimately never played for the Fever.<ref>{{cite web |title=2000 WNBA Expansion Draft |url=https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/draft/2000-expansion.html |website=Basketball Reference |access-date=June 24, 2025}}</ref>
