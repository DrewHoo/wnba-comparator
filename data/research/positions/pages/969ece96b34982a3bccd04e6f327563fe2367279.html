Career Highlights
Played fourth season of professional ball and first for BBK Ockelbo, Sweden during 1996-97 season
Suited up for parts of four seasons in four different countries
Played two seasons for KK Vozdovac in Belgrade, Serbia (1983-85), won one Yugoslavian Cup championship
Left the professional ranks first for the Yugoslav National Team and then to spend time with her family until 1995-96, when she played for Fribourg, Switzerland
Named to the Kodak All-America team in her senior season at Maryland in 1983
A two-time ACC Tournament MVP in 1982 and 1983
Named to the West Regional All-Tournament team in 1982, as Maryland reached the Final Four
Her uniform, No. 4, was retired by the Terrapins
A two-time Olympian and member of the Yugoslav National Team from 1979-87

WNBA Transactions
Selected by the New York Liberty in local tryouts, 5/22/97

Personal
Nickname is "Jazz"
Balances playing career with growing career as a sports agent for her own agency, Perazic International, which represents about 30 athletes, mostly women
Most of her favorite activities away from the court involve racing, especially motorcycles
Married for nine years to Robert Gipe
Love for motorcycles is surpassed by love of her family and the couple's 6-year-old daughter, Deanna
Besides English, speaks Serbian, German and some Italian
