'''Markita Uran Aldridge''' (born September 15, 1973) is an American former [[basketball]] [[shooting guard]]. Aldridge played three seasons with the [[Washington Mystics]] from 1999 to 2001. During her time with Washington, she averaged 2.3 points, one rebound, and one assist per game over 10.6 minutes.<ref name="bbr">{{cite web|url=https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/players/a/aldrima01w.html|title=Markita Aldridge|publisher=basketball-reference.com|accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref>

Aldridge attended [[University of North Carolina at Charlotte]], where she played with the [[Charlotte 49ers women's basketball|49ers]]. She graduated from Charlotte in 1996.<ref name="Knight">{{Cite news |last=Knight |first=Athelia |date=June 10, 1999 |title=Mystics' Holdsclaw Is the Future of WNBA |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-srv/sports/wnba/longterm/preview99/stories/profiles10.htm |access-date=3 May 2018 |work=[[The Washington Post]]}}</ref>

==Personal life==
In March 2017, Aldridge became the first former WNBA player appointed to the finance committee of the [[National Basketball Retired Players Association]], which is the official alumni organization for multiple professional basketball leagues in the United States.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.legendsofbasketball.com/former-wnba-great-markita-aldridge-woods-becomes-first-woman-on-nbrpa-finance-committee/|title=MARKITA ALDRIDGE-WOODS BECOMES FIRST WOMAN ON NBRPA FINANCE COMMITTEE|date=March 23, 2017|publisher=legendsofbasketball.com|accessdate=3 May 2018|archive-date=5 October 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181005153725/https://www.legendsofbasketball.com/former-wnba-great-markita-aldridge-woods-becomes-first-woman-on-nbrpa-finance-committee/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
