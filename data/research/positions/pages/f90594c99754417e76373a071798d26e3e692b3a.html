1997 Notes
Placed on the injured list on June 24 with a back injury.

Career Highlights
Played 1996-97 season in Berlin, Germany for Wemex
Averaged 15 points, eight assists and six rebounds per game
Joined Wemex in 1993
Was three-time All-Star player in Germany
Started her professional basketball career in 1987 with Red Star, a club team in her hometown of Belgrade, Yugoslavia
Interrupted European career to come to United States for college
Is most decorated player in Cal women's basketball history
Ranks second all-time in career scoring average (15.7), fifth in assists (358) and third in three-point shooting percentage (.389)
Named as Cal's Female Basketball Athlete of the Decade
During senior season (1993) was named to Kodak All-America team, Basketball Times team, All-Pac 10 team and Cal MVP
Led team in scoring, assists and steals
Sports Illustrated National Player of the Week, March 2, 1992

WNBA Transactions
Selected by the Charlotte Sting in local tryouts, 05/22/97

Personal
Graduated from Berkeley with a degree in Economics and also has a degree in Management from the Advanced Business College in Belgrade, Yugoslavia
Married last August to Mirko Culik, a professional volleyball player currently in Germany
Youngest of three sisters
Speaks English, German, Serbian, Croatian and Bosnian
Met and became friends with Charlotte Hornets' Vlade Divac while both were playing basketball in home country of Yugoslavia
Superstitions include drinking a cup of coffee and eating a couple of cookies before a game
Hobbies include reading, going to the movies, riding her bike, listening to classical music and attending the opera
